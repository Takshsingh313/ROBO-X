COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Code of Conduct

Fostering an inclusive, respectful, and innovative robotics community at Chandigarh University.

1. Respect & Inclusivity

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free environment for everyone regardless of gender, skill level, background, or identity. Respect fellow builders and collaborators at all times.

2. Collaborative Learning

Share knowledge, mentor junior members, and handle hardware/lab equipment with care and safety. Curiosity and constructive feedback are encouraged.

3. Integrity & Safety

Adhere to Chandigarh University rules, lab safety procedures, and open-source project ethics. Violations will be handled by club coordinators.
